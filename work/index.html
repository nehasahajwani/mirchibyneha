I was part of the MIT-MAHE team that competed in iGEM 2025, an international synthetic biology competition and forum where teams from around the world develop meaningful solutions to real-world problems. On our project, PhytClub, I worked across research, visual design, and marketing. The role opened up new creative avenues for me, from hand-drawn animation to scientific illustration and writing. A key part of the visual identity involved weaving my Indian culture into the brand while keeping the science clear and accessible.

Digitally designed postcard, created as merch for MIT-MAHE's iGEM '25. The centrepiece features original photography of a landmark temple from Manipal, the team's home city, celebrating Indian heritage through design.

Clarity by Design: A guide to project graphics

Independently written and designed, exploring how visual clarity strengthens scientific communication, created as a contribution to MIT-MAHE's iGEM '25. While working on the branding and visual direction of the project, our team lacked guidance on designing the wiki, scientific illustrations for protocols and diagrams, presentations, and other visual assets, all while trying to keep them cohesive and visually engaging. This guide addresses that gap by offering clear design guidance and examples, including the complete set of fonts and colour palettes used by the team. Structured around my own experience and understanding of design, it serves as a practical reference for translating scientific work into compelling visuals.

Designed On: Canva

Munchi: A Collection of Recipes Starring Black Pepper

Munchi (Black Pepper in Tulu) is a recipe book featuring dishes from across India that star the spice. The cover features hand-painted, traditionally inspired tile artwork, scanned and digitally composited to draw on Indian heritage in its visual identity. The recipes are authentic from Indian households. From concept to final layout, every element, assembly, design, and formatting was independently developed.

Designed and Formatted On: Canva

Cover Art: Hand-painted using watercolour, photographed on Canon EOS M50, edited on Lightroom

Kaalumenasu: Mascot of PhytClub, MIT-MAHE '25

Kaalumenasu is the mascot of PhytClub 2025, a common hill myna found in black pepper plantations, the crop our project targeted to protect. Her name translates to "Black Pepper" from Kannada, the language of our origin city. She is the face of our project, appearing in our wiki, all our presentations, stickers and in the form of a plushie we kept at our booth at the competition. The mascot was conceptualised and hand-drawn independently.

Illustrated On: Procreate
