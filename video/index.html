I capture everyday life and the quiet, authentic moments that make up the world around me. My work leans into a nostalgic, film-like aesthetic, warm tones and soft texture that mirror how memory itself works. As an emerging videographer and editor, I'm still refining my craft, one project at a time.

Paris 2025

A 3 minutes and 14 seconds ode to a city that called, inspired, and left me awestruck, a once in a lifetime chance to experience Paris in my 20s. Deliberate handheld camcorder effect on mirrorless, blending high-quality footage with nostalgia.

Filming: Canon EOS M50

Software: Edited, mixed, and captioned on VN

PhytClub iGEM Wiki Freeze '25

Recording moments before the awaited wiki freeze for iGEM 2025, an international SynBio competition. A room buzzing with anticipation for the wait to finally be over. A whole year's hard work, making or breaking in the final seconds.

Filming: Canon EOS M50

Software: Edited, mixed, and captioned on VN
